Privacy Policy – Lethal Tower

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Publisher and data controller

The mobile game Lethal Tower (“the Game”) is published by Baud Prod, a French SASU registered with the Mulhouse Trade and Companies Register under number 992699876, with its registered office at 1 RUE MARGUERIN 75014 PARIS (“we”, “us”).

Contact: Devbaudopro@outlook.com

2. What we do not collect

The Game requires no account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files, and we do not sell any data.

3. Data stored locally

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device and are never sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

4. Advertising

The Game displays ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve these ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, this partner may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location (country, city), derived from the IP address;
  • technical device information (model, operating system, language, carrier);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

This data is collected and processed directly by Google AdMob under its own privacy policy. We do not have access to it in a form that identifies you.

Your consent. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form is shown when you first launch the Game. You can accept or refuse personalized ads there. If you refuse, non-personalized ads based only on context may still be shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings > Privacy menu.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your bank or payment details. We only receive the information needed to validate the purchase and deliver the purchased content: order ID, product purchased, and purchase date and status. Payment processing is subject to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The in-app purchase module is provided by Unity Technologies, which may process technical data (installation ID, device information) to make purchases work: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

6. Legal bases

  • Personalized ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Non-personalized ads and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding the Game (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • In-app purchases: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

7. Transfers outside the European Union

Our partners (Google, Unity, Google AdMob) may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States. These transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Retention

Data is kept by our partners for the periods stated in their respective policies. Progress data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will take the necessary steps.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have in particular the following rights:

  • access, correction and deletion of your data;
  • objection to processing and restriction of processing;
  • withdrawal of your consent at any time.

The Game does not allow us to identify you. For data collected by our partners, you can exercise your rights directly with them, or contact us and we will direct you. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

11. Changes

We may update this policy, in particular when we add a new feature or partner. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
